# Map markers

WORK_QUEUE item 53. Owner, 2026-11-12: **"Let's use non-Unicode vector style symbols to let you
annotate your own map so you can find your way around."** / **"Add that to the queue."**

## The owner's answers

- **Where they show:** the big map (M), the minimap, and in the world — a waypoint over the spot with
  its distance.
- **Whose they are:** private to the player, saved with the world for that player.
- **What a marker is:** an icon from a set, a colour, a short label.
- **Auto markers:** spawn, last death, portals once seen.
- **Icons are vector** (SVG path data, drawn by the canvas or as inline SVG), never emoji or Unicode
  glyphs.

## The data (`shared/ui/markers.json`)

One file, validated and generated by `shared/ui/gen.mjs` into `client/src/ui/markers.gen.ts` (the
shell) and `crates/dwell-protocol/src/markers.gen.rs` (the limits the server holds a record to); CI
fails on stale output (`shared/ui/gen.test.mjs`).

- `icons`: `id` (the name a marker saves), `name` (the picker's), `colour` (its default, a palette
  name) and `path`: SVG path data in a 24 × 24 box, absolute `M L H V C Q A Z` only, every point
  inside the box (the validator walks the path), filled even-odd so holes are inner subpaths. A new
  icon is one entry; the test adds a fixture icon by data alone.
- `palette`: the colours offered. A marker saves its colour itself (#rrggbb), so a changed palette
  leaves placed markers as they are.
- `auto`: the kinds the game places — `spawn` (where the player first came into the world), `death`
  (where they last died, moved on each death), `portal` (each portal opening once seen, on its
  face; "The rest" below) — each `on`, `keep` (`one`: a single marker moved to the newest place;
  `each`: one per place, a place within 2 m across the ground counting as seen), its icon, colour and label.
- `limits`: the most markers per player per world (auto ones included), a label's most characters
  (code points, as Rust's `chars`), the most bytes of an icon or kind name, the big map's pick radius.
- `world`: the waypoint's size, its fade (full to `nearM`, `fadedAlpha` from `farM`), the edge margin
  an off-screen waypoint clamps to, `showM` (farther markers show only on the maps) and `hideWithinM` (a marker
  this near the eye or across the ground, at or under the camera, shows no waypoint; BUGS.md #116).

What stays code: the path vocabulary, the two keep rules, where the auto kinds are noted (the shell
notes `spawn`, `death` and the portals the client crate sees, `map_markers::seen_portals`).

## Filters (0.58)

The big map's filter button lists the layers the maps show (`client/src/ui/mapShrines.ts`
`filterLayers`): the resurrection shrines ([SHRINES.md](SHRINES.md), a derived layer drawn with the
`shrine` icon, not saved markers), each `auto` kind by data, and the player's own markers. A hidden
layer leaves the big map, the minimap and the world waypoints, and its markers aren't picked;
the choices are per browser (`dwell.map.filters`).

## The record and the wire

`dwell_protocol::markers::Markers` (and `client/src/ui/markers.ts`'s codec, both pinned by the golden
vector `markers` in `shared/protocol/vectors.txt`): `u8 version` 1, `u16 count`, each marker `u32 id`,
`str icon`, `u8 r g b`, `str label`, `u8 face`, `f32 x y z` (face-local: the face's up is +y, through
`FaceFrame::point`), `str auto` ('' the player's own). Ids are unique; names this build doesn't know
are kept (an unknown icon draws as the fallback).

- **Saved** in the world's optional `markers` table, keyed by device key (ARCHITECTURE.md §6.4).
  Additive: an older build on the line never opens it, so no new compatibility line.
- **Synced** behind the `markers.private` capability (ARCHITECTURE.md §8.3): the server sends a player
  their own record once after their `Capabilities` (`Markers` 0x6C), never anyone else's; the client
  sends its whole record on each change. Without the capability the markers last the session.
- **Local worlds** run the same server in the browser, so they keep markers the same way, in the
  world's own file.

## The shell

`client/src/ui/markers.ts` holds `MarkerList` (place, edit, move, delete, the limits, `noteAuto`),
the codec and the geometry; `markerPicker.ts` the big map's picker (icons as SVG, the palette, a
label, Save / Delete / Cancel; `PickerDraft` its rules); `minimap.ts` draws them:

- **Big map:** a click on empty ground opens the picker to place a marker there, a pending pin (the
  picker's icon and colour, ringed) marking the spot until it is saved or cancelled (BUGS.md #117); a click on a marker
  opens it to edit or delete; dragging a marker moves it. Markers on the shown face are drawn.
- **Minimap:** markers on the player's face within the disc, heading up (`minimapOffset`, the same
  frame as `dwell_game::minimap::MapFrame`; face B's right is the mirror's).
- **World:** a waypoint (icon, label, distance) over each marker on the player's face within `showM`,
  placed by `Client.markerScreen` (the renderer's camera, `render::camera::project`), faded by
  distance and clamped to the screen's edge off screen or behind. It is a HUD part (`hud.json`
  `waypoints`), so Settings → On screen can hide it.

## Tests

- `shared/ui/gen.test.mjs`: the data validates and generates the checked-in output; a fixture icon
  by data alone; bad entries (an icon twice, an unknown colour, a point outside the box, a relative
  command, a glyph, an unknown auto icon) fail naming them.
- `dwell-protocol`: the record round-trips, unknown names survive, bad records are refused; the
  golden vector and its malformed ones.
- `dwell-storage`: each player's record is their own; a world without the table has none.
- `dwell-sim` `tests/markers.rs` (both faces): a player's markers go to them alone, are saved with the
  world and come back at the next join as placed; a client without the capability is sent none and
  can't set them.
- `dwell-client` `render::camera` (both faces): the waypoint's projection.
- `client/src/ui/markers.test.ts`: the codec against the golden vector, the list's rules and limits,
  the auto kinds (one moved, one each, a kind by data alone), the picker's draft, the minimap offset
  on both faces, picking, the waypoint's fade and edge clamp.

## The rest (0.61)

- **The markers list:** the big map's list button opens a side panel (hidden while the picker is open)
  of the markers the filters show (`markerRows`): each its icon, label, distance from the player and an
  arrow the way to it as the big map shows it (+z up; face A's +x left, face B's right), nearest first,
  the other face's after ("other side"). A row centres the map on its marker (`BigMapState.centerOn`);
  its buttons edit (the picker) or delete it.
- **Portals:** the client crate's `map_markers::seen_portals` (`Client.seenPortals`) gives each portal
  opening on the player's face within the render radius (`RENDER_RADIUS_CHUNKS` chunks, across the
  ground), standing at its opening's height (the rim's top) from the generator's shafts (`world.portals`,
  known from the seed wherever the player is), else the loaded rim's top, else NaN, when the shell takes
  the map's height as a provisional guess, or notes nothing yet: never the player's height (BUGS.md #153).
  The shell notes each, a few times a second, as `noteAuto('portal', …, settled)`; `each` counts a place
  within 2 m **across the ground** as seen, so a re-read makes no second marker, and a settled height
  (the opening's) moves the marker there, which corrects a saved one at a wrong height. A portal's two
  openings are two markers, one per face.
- **The ground under a placed marker:** a marker placed or dragged on the big map stands at
  `markerGroundY`: the loaded column's top there (`Client.markerGround`, `dwell_game::minimap::ground_at`
  read round the eye, face-local), else the map's height for its cell (the finest level's, else the
  explored cell's; a water cell's is its bed's), else the player's height on that face. The waypoint
  then floats 1.5 m over the ground there. A marker placed or dragged where the column wasn't loaded is
  provisional (for the session) and is put back on its ground once the column loads; one standing more
  than `world.regroundAboveM` (8 m) over its loaded ground (one saved before this fix at the player's
  height) is too (`regroundMarkers`, within the render radius, a few times a second).

Tests: `dwell-game` `a_columns_ground_is_its_top_blocks_top_face_locally` and `dwell-client`
`map_markers` (both faces); `markers.test.ts`' list rows against the big map's `toScreen` on both
faces, the ground's fallbacks, and seen portals noted once per opening.
