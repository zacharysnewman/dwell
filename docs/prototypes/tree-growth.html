Tree Growth

Three species as data, grown from a thin sapling to the mature tree: the same silhouette at every stage, the trunk thickening in ¼ m steps. Leaves in the chosen Leaf Lab look (option 5) with the game's tiles; octagonal logs squared where they meet (ADR 0072). Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. The earlier skeleton-reveal tool (Phase 30 G0) is tree-growth-skeleton.html.

Seeds. Each seed draws its own mature height (oak 3.5–7 m, pine 7–14 m, palm 4.5–9.5 m of trunk), a crown size (×0.8–1.2) and a leaf tint (lighter or darker, a slight hue shift): variety in the species data. Try Seeds.

      
Growth as data. Each species has growth.stages; a tree's stage is a small integer the game could store per tree
      (or derive from its age), and every stage is a pure function of (species, seed, stage). Height grows in ½ m steps (the half-height grid),
      the trunk's width in ¼ m steps (an octagon of half-width 0.125–0.5 m), the crown is the mature crown scaled. Oak branches appear when
      the trunk is thick enough to carry them: the skeleton reveal of ADR 0033, kept where it fits.

      
Blocks in game. Oak and pine crowns are 1 m leaf blocks (collision, breaking, placing), drawn chamfered with canopy normals and
      painted cards. Palm fronds can be drawn two ways (Palm fronds, and Split
      for the two side by side, the same palm from the same camera): leaf blocks, as in the game today (0.52.58): each frond's path
      sampled 12 times and every ½ m cell it crosses a dwell:leaves block, chamfered and carded like any other leaf; or
      frond cards: a few long arching painted cards along each frond's path (two to four by its length), the leaf card's painterly
      strokes in a frond's shape, the droop growing with the frond's length. As cards, each frond would be cosmetic, owned by the palm's
      crown block, and the cells it passes through palm_frond blocks with no collision you can still break (shown with
      Show block cells). The palm's lean is stored as ⅛ m offsets per ½ m trunk segment (a log shape property, not in the game yet).

      
Content is prototype: block names marked proposed are not in the registry yet, and nothing here is in the game.
