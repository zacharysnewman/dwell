Dwell · tree growth prototype

Tree Growth

A hand-built tree is the mature target. Its wood is read as a skeleton, and growth reveals that skeleton from the root: the trunk 1 m every 30 in-game days, branches at a third of that, thickening by the pipe model. The last stage is the model, voxel for voxel.

    A trunk node appears at its height × days per metre; a branch node at its fork's time plus its distance from the fork ÷ (trunk rate × ratio). The world speed divides every time (a per-world setting, not structure data).

    Obstacles

    Turn on, then click a voxel face or the ground to place a stone; click a stone to remove it. A node whose core runs into a stone is pruned with everything beyond it.

    Branch angle

    Each branch rotates about its fork (around the parent's axis, and tilts by half as much), scales its length, bends down by the droop per metre, or is dropped. Leaves move with their nodes; the trunk, roots and anything holding a core voxel stay rigid.
