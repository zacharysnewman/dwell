      1. Top. A corner of a natural top voxel stays at the voxel's top.
      2. Drop. If any of the 3 columns around the corner is lower, it drops to the voxel's floor (with Top 1 m, down to that column's height, at most 1 m).
      3. One voxel wide. When every corner drops, a ridge runs from the centre to each same-height edge neighbour: pyramid, ridge end, ridge, corner, T, cross.
      4. Ramp up. When every corner drops and exactly one edge neighbour is higher (none the same), the voxel is a wedge rising to it.

      No Rest or Rise rule: with ½ m cells, flat ground already ends on a cell boundary, so nothing needs to rise into the cell above.
