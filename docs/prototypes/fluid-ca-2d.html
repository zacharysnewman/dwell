Conserved Fluid Logic

Water is never created or destroyed by the rules: every move is a swap. Only the brush adds or removes it.

Materials
Water rules (in order)
  1. Fall into an empty cell below.
  2. Slide down a diagonal (only past an open side, so it can't squeeze through a wall's corner).
  3. Otherwise look along its row, through empty cells, for the nearest place it can fall, and step toward it.
  4. No place to fall: stay still. (A lone droplet rests instead of being deleted.)
Each cell moves at most once a generation.
