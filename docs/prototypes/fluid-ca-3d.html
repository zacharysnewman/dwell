Fluid CA 3D

Falling-sand water in 3D: each voxel is empty, sand, water or stone, and every move is a swap, so the rules never create or destroy water. Water settles: it rests when there is nowhere lower to go.

Water rules, in order
  1. Fall into an empty cell below.
  2. Slide down a diagonal, past an open side (never through a wall's corner).
  3. Step toward the nearest place in its layer, through empty cells, where it can fall (a distance field per layer, so it finds its way round corners).
  4. Nowhere lower to go: rest. A lone droplet rests rather than skating forever.

Each cell moves at most once a generation. Only the brush, springs (cyan, give a cell of water a generation into an empty neighbour) and drains (dark, remove water touching them) change the amount of water; the numbers count them. Not modelled: pressure (water won't rise up the far side of a U-bend).
