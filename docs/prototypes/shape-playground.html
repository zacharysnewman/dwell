Conflicts

    Your edits are saved in this browser as you go. Apply rules to all replaces every block's shape; Your last export brings back the shapes you sent on 8 October; the new situations get the rules' shapes.

    Shapes

    The game's shapes (shared/blocks/shapes.mjs) plus the shapes the rules call for that the game doesn't have yet: 12 corner shapes (half-and-half, tilts, saddles and others) and the half pyramid and half ridge pieces. Slopes face the way they descend; a ridge end faces the way its ridge runs, and a ridge corner turns from that way clockwise.
