# 0039. The spyglass: a data-driven zoom whose detail follows the narrow view

- Status: Accepted (2026-10-19: the owner's answers to the design questions, `SPYGLASS.md` §1)
- Date: 2026-10-19
- Builds on: [0029](0029-ray-marched-renderer.md) (the renderer seam and compare mode),
  Phase 29's motion effects (the frame's FOV given to the renderer and the LOD alike)
- Design: [`SPYGLASS.md`](../SPYGLASS.md)

## Context

The owner asked for a spyglass (2026-10-19) to see how the ray marcher and the LOD compare far
away: a narrow view that shows more detail in a smaller cone. Players get it too, with the raster
renderer. The frame's vertical FOV already reached the renderer and the LOD selection (the speed
boost), but the LOD refines in every direction by distance alone, so a narrower FOV would refine the
whole world; and the marcher took its rays and cone LOD from the screen's own FOV, not the frame's.

## Options considered

- **What a step names.** (a) A vertical FOV in degrees; (b) a magnification of the frame's FOV.
  (a) zooms differently at each Field of view setting and screen shape; (b) is one zoom everywhere and
  is the factor the pixel size changes by.
- **Detail in the zoomed view.** (a) The narrow FOV everywhere: the whole world refines to the zoomed
  pixel size (16–64× the sections); (b) the narrow FOV only inside the view, the unzoomed one outside;
  (c) no extra detail (the zoom shows the same sections larger). (c) defeats the purpose; (a) blows
  the memory budget; (b) adds about `(h-FOV / m) / 360° × detail²` of the view's sections.
- **Bounding it.** Rely on the cache budget's error scale alone (it coarsens the whole view, and
  recovers slowly), or a cap on the detail magnification as data, with the cache as the hard limit.
- **Activation.** Hold or toggle (the owner: toggle); steps by repeated presses or by the wheel.
- **Vignette.** In the composite shader (per renderer) or a DOM layer over the view (one for every
  renderer, data-driven, no shader change while the marcher is being optimized).

## Decision

- Tunables in `shared/look/spyglass.json` (key, magnification steps, detail boost, transition time,
  vignette), validated and generated by `shared/look/spyglass.mjs` into `dwell-look` and the shell's
  controls table, CI stale check; a step is a magnification (b).
- The frame's FOV is the zoomed one: `zoom_fov(effective_fov(base, boost), magnification)`, given to
  the raster renderer, the marcher (`march_frame` now uses the frame's FOV) and the LOD camera.
- The LOD camera carries the magnification and the detail magnification (`min(m, detailBoost)`); a
  section inside the view is measured at the detail's pixel size, outside at the unzoomed FOV's (b).
  The cache budget stays the hard limit.
- A toggle key (`` ` ``), the wheel steps while zoomed, mouse look scaled by 1/magnification, an
  eased transition in log space (instant under reduced motion), a DOM vignette.
- No change to saves, the protocol or terrain: a patch release.

## Consequences

- The LOD's rule "detail depends on distance alone" has one exception, the zoomed cone; unzooming
  leaves its finer sections in the cache to be evicted as usual.
- More steps, another key, a different vignette or a tighter detail bound are data edits.
- Turning while zoomed loads the new cone's detail; the old cone's stays until evicted.
- Reversal: drop the data file, the key and the two `LodCamera` fields; the marcher's FOV fix stays.
