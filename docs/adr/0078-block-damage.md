# ADR 0078: Block damage — hits to break by tool, as block data

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-10; D1 built in 0.52.28)

## Context

The owner (2026-11-10): "Tools will do damage to blocks, blocks will retain damage until destroyed or
repaired." Pickaxes break shards off stone, shovels scoop soil. Answers: the damage is a look only
(collision stays the full block); each hit drops some of the resource and the destroy the other half;
hits are tiered (the right tool at stone tier about 5, metal about 3, the wrong tool or bare hands about
10–12); bare hands can't break stone; the same in creative and adventure; "Blocks will need per tool
and default values". The design is [`docs/BLOCK_DAMAGE.md`](../BLOCK_DAMAGE.md).

## Decision

- Each block's `damage` (block data): `default` (bare hands or an unlisted tool; `null`: can't) and
  hits per tool class or item, an item's over its class's; a class's count is divided by the held
  item's tier speed (`tools.json` `breaking.tiers`). Generated by `shared/tools/gen.mjs` for Rust
  (`dwell_protocol::chopping::BLOCK_DAMAGE`, resolved by `dwell_protocol::damage::hits_to_break`) and TS.
  A block without it breaks in one hit.
- The server keeps per damaged cell the state hit, the hits and up to four hit marks
  (`dwell_protocol::damage::Damage`); a hit short of the count leaves the block and acks the edit as
  refused; the last hit breaks it. Half the block's certain drops are spread over the hits, the rest on
  the destroy, totalling the block's drops.
- **Compatibility:** the damage is saved in a new optional table (`block_damage`) of the world file;
  older builds on the 0.52 line never open it, a world without it loads undamaged. No protocol message
  changes in D1 (the client learns the counts from the same data; the damage itself is sent to clients
  in D2 with its look). Not a breaking change: no new compatibility line.

## Consequences

- Breaking stone, soil and sand takes several clicks; e2e specs that break with one click hit until the
  cell breaks; native tests use `ServerConfig::one_hit_breaks`.
- A break that takes several hits is not predicted on the client; it shows when the server's change
  arrives.
- With today's one-item drops a hit drops nothing until a block drops more (tuning, D3).
