# 0080 — Screen-space ambient occlusion as its own passes, on the ambient share

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-15

## Context

Owner, 2026-11-15: "I'd like to implement screen space ambient occlusion." Answers: on the High and
Ultra presets (half resolution on High within ~1 ms, full on Ultra; never on phones or a CPU adapter);
everything, soft, without doubling the baked corner shading; data for the look and a Settings toggle and
strength (docs/SSAO.md §1). The renderer is forward: the composite sees only the lit colour and the view
depth, there is no normal buffer, and the composite already binds the 16 sampled textures a shader stage
may have.

## Options considered

1. **Read the occlusion in the composite.** Needs a 17th sampled texture in the composite: over the
   WebGPU default limit.
2. **A depth prepass and the occlusion read in the scene shaders.** Applies it to the ambient term
   exactly, but doubles the geometry cost: far over budget on the M1.
3. **Separate passes that multiply the resolved colour by the occlusion × each pixel's ambient share**,
   the share written by the scene's lighting into spare low bits of the view depth. One extra
   full-size pass, its own bind group, no prepass; the multiply by the share is the same as scaling the
   ambient term alone.

## Decision

Option 3: `render/ssao.rs` — occlusion at the tier's resolution from the view depth (normals
reconstructed), a depth-aware separable blur, and an apply pass that multiplies the scene's resolved
colour (blend Zero, Src) by 1 − (1 − AO) × share, after the scene passes and the arms and before the
clouds, shafts, bloom and composite. The share (the hemisphere and block light's part of the fragment's
irradiance, weakened where the baked shading darkens it) lives in the view depth's bits 2–4. Look and
tiers are data (`shared/look/ssao.json`); the tier and strength are a setting (`dwell.ssao`).

## Consequences

- The view depth's low five bits are now flags and the share; every writer already goes through
  `frame_depth` (tested), and five ulps are far below what any reader resolves.
- Water, the sky and self-lit surfaces take none by construction (share 0). Under water the floor's share
  applies to the water pixel (docs/SSAO.md §5).
- Off costs nothing: no pipelines, 1 × 1 buffers. Reversing it is removing the passes and the bits.
